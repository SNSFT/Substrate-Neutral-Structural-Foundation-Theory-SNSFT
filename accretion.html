Merger product by the 2-beam rule of the GAM Collider v15 (Geometric Axiomatic Module Collider) [9,9,2,3.8]: P = P₁P₂/(P₁+P₂) · N = N₁+N₂ · B = |B₁−B₂| · A = max(A₁,A₂). A body with τ ≥ TL reorganizes: it splits into two halves with P doubled and N halved (each half carries τ/2), repeated until each piece sits below TL. Mass and momentum are conserved.
COLLISIONS
Two bodies that touch merge if the impact is slow enough, otherwise they glance off each other (hit-and-run). The dividing speed vcr comes from the collision simulations of Kokubo & Genda (2010) and depends on the mass ratio and the impact angle. Boundary identification: τc = TL × vimp / vcr, so a collision merges below TL and hits and runs at or above it.
vcr/vesc = 2.43Γ²Θ5/2 − 0.0408Γ + 1.86Θ5/2 + 1.08, Γ = (M₁−M₂)/(M₁+M₂), Θ = 1 − sin θ. vesc = √(2G(M₁+M₂)/(R₁+R₂)) with physical radii; vimp = √(v∞² + vesc²), v∞ taken from the encounter at the enhanced contact distance.
