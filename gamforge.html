P — heavy atoms holding the shape (backbone 4 + side chain). A count.
N — side-chain χ dihedral count. Ponder & Richards (1987) J Mol Biol 193, 775.
B — side-chain H-bond capacity, donors + acceptors. A count.

SCOPE. Structural screen. Reports whether a set closes and what coupling the closure needs. Not binding, activity, selectivity, absorption or safety — those are measured, never derived.
SHATTER is not absence. It is unsatisfied coupling and faster reorganization. Iron rusts; the rust is iron reorganizing. The measurable counterpart here is solvent exchange — unsatisfied hydrogen bonds exchange faster, which is what hydrogen-deuterium exchange protection factors measure.
