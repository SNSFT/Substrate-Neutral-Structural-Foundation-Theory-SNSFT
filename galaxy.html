GRAVITY · SELF-PULL + FUSION
Stars pull on each other (Newton's G, masses from the measured disk Σ(R)) and move freely. The rotation curve fixes the mean pull; self-gravity adds the local lumps. Two stars fuse by the GAMCollider 2-beam rule [9,9,2,3.8] only where they are close, bound, approaching, and their neighbourhood is gravitationally unstable (local Toomre Q < 1, measured live from the stars in each 2 kpc cell), the standard sink-particle criteria.
Fusion: P = P₁P₂/(P₁+P₂) · N = N₁+N₂ · B = |B₁−B₂| · A = max(A₁,A₂). Mass and momentum are conserved. A product with τ ≥ TL shatters and is ejected.
