Every measured contact between the corpus and the humans and AI systems that reach it. Works sit on the inner ring: papers, figshare items, site pages and repositories. Around them sit the identities that made contact: human readers by city and institution, places that downloaded, AI operators that crawled and fetched, and outside systems that cloned. Each line is an L = (4)(2) pair, two identities in behavioral contact. Identity Mass is the running total of those contacts and only grows; the day slider replays it from genesis.
