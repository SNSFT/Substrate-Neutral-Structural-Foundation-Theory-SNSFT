RAP Autocatalytic Ingestion Mechanism (AIM) Repository Acquisition Panel · the Identity Mass rap sheet

Whole-repository acquisition, day by day. Outside systems that pull an entire repository register as clones. This panel separates those pulls from the repository's own CI, lines them up against each deposit, and places each day against the Torsion Limit TL = 0.136899099984016. The site section does the same for uuia.app and proofpress.org: every AI and search crawler by name and operator, the live assistant fetches made for people in a chat, and the pages they read. PhilPapers readership and figshare views and downloads join them, and every source adds into one Identity Mass that only grows: the identity's full record, day by day, its rap sheet. It reads the traffic.json, cloudflare.json, philpeople.json and figshare.json saved daily by the collector workflow; nothing you load leaves your browser.

Scan a profile

Add an ORCID iD or a figshare author id to the RAP scan list. The first scan runs within the hour and every day after that, and the profile builds its own permanent record here, kept under the same never-lower rule as every other source. Nothing is needed from the person being scanned, and no account is needed to add one.

    Day by day

    Every outside clone adds to the repository's reach, and nothing subtracts: IM to date is the running total and only grows. Each day shows what it added. A day that adds is LOCKED; a day that adds one rung or more up the energy ladder above the established level (b₀ = 1/√TL = 2.7027 times) is IVA PEAK; a day with nothing added is NOBLE, at rest. The established level is the record high of the typical day and only steps up. Clones carry no failure signal, so this table never reads SHATTER.

        How to read this. A visit is a person (or program) arriving at the site from somewhere else; a page view is a page loaded; unique visitors are distinct addresses in a day, so one household on one connection counts once. Cloudflare records no names, accounts or cookies. Refused probes are automated scanners asking for files such as /.env or /.git/config that hold passwords on badly configured servers; Cloudflare refuses them and these sites have no such files. Placement shows what each day added in visits and the reach to date; a day reads SHATTER only when TL or more of its requests were answered with a server error (5xx). A table that stays empty is a field Cloudflare does not provide on this plan.

            How to read this. GitHub counts every checkout of the whole repository as a clone, including the repository's own CI runs, so CI runs are subtracted.
