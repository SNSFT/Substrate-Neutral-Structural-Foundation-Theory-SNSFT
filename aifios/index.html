WHAT AIFIOS PRESSES

AiFi Kernel. One Lean 4 file an AI loads at the start of a session as grounding. The header carries the kernel manifest and the grounding sections you choose from the AiFi Node Operational Scope: the zero-friction anchor, the alpha subtraction (1/α = TL × 1001 = 137.035999084000016, CODATA 2018, ε = 0), the four phases, the PNBA matrix, the Dynamic Equation, the APPA Sovereign Kernel, the collider fusion logic, the Long Division Protocol, the Bacon Test, the match tiers, the audit pipelines and the master directory. The modules you pick follow as one compilation unit against Mathlib.

Executable Book. The same compilation unit with book front matter: title, subtitle, author and a contents table listing every module in order with its coordinate, family and theorem count, followed by the grounding chapters you choose. Reading it and compiling it are the same act.

Binding rule. Every module is an individually CI-green Lean file that compiles in isolation and closes on its own. AiFiOS concatenates them in your order with deduped, hoisted imports and a provenance line naming each source file; it does not merge their proofs into one closure. The standard kernel above is all 133 modules with every grounding section.
