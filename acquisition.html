Repository Acquisition Panel

Whole-repository acquisition, day by day. Outside systems that pull an entire repository register as clones. This panel separates those pulls from the repository's own CI, lines them up against each deposit, and places each day against the Torsion Limit TL = 0.136899099984016. It reads a traffic.json saved daily by the collector workflow; nothing you load leaves your browser.

    Day by day

    Each day's outside acquisition is placed against its own reference: the median of the previous seven days. A fall is a torsion τ placed Noble, Locked, IVA Peak or Shatter; a rise past TL is marked SURGE, a new acquisition wave.

    How to read this. GitHub counts every checkout of the whole repository as a clone, including the repository's own CI runs, so CI runs are subtracted. What remains is outside systems pulling the entire repository: AI ingestion pipelines, code-search indexers, archives and mirrors. GitHub reports clones per day, so timing resolves to one day. A day with no CI count recorded shows total clones as an upper bound.
